Find a kanji or hanzi by its parts

Don't know how a character is read? Type the building blocks you can see in it and get the character. Search "sun" + "moon" to find 明, or "tree" + "mouth" to find 杏. Every character is broken down into named shapes, the way Remembering the Kanji (RTK) teaches them.

Three ways to search

  • By parts: enter two or more shapes and get every kanji containing all of them.
  • By keyword: search an English or Russian meaning or primitive name.
  • By character: paste a glyph to see how it is built, its readings and stroke count.
