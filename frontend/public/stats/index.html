What the numbers mean

Real people are visitors who ran a search or opened a kanji in the app. Crawlers and bot bursts load the page and leave, so they never appear there. Page views and visitors count every load of the app that did not identify itself as a bot, so they still include bots that pretend to be browsers (on some days thousands, one view each). Searches and kanji opened count page loads in which that happened. Readers of the static /kanji/… pages who never open the app are not counted.
